English

This policy covers the iOS App Store edition of Raincheck Workshop (bundle ID: com.chenzhifeng.raincheckworkshop), provided by chen zhifeng. It does not cover an Android advertising edition or Apple’s own services.

Game data stays in the app’s storage

The game stores repair progress, cloth layouts, completed work, settings and practice pieces in its local app storage. It has no game-account registration, developer-operated cloud-save service or upload feature for these records. The game does not transmit these records to us.

The app does not include advertising, a third-party analytics SDK or a developer-operated crash-reporting upload service. It does not request your name, email address, contacts, location, camera, microphone or advertising identifier for gameplay.

Purchases and Apple

Apple’s StoreKit service handles the full-story purchase and restoration. The game checks Apple-verified entitlement and transaction information, including the product and transaction identifiers, purchase environment and entitlement status. It stores a local delivery record so that an interrupted unlock can be reconciled. The game does not send this local record to a developer server.

We do not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account password through the game. Apple processes purchase, account and device information under its own terms and App Store privacy notice. Apple may make purchase/refund records and aggregated reporting available to developers. Apple’s processing is separate from the game’s local storage.

If you contact support

When you choose to email 610186215@qq.com, we receive your sender address and anything you include, such as your message, device details or an optional screenshot. We use this information to respond to your request and resolve the issue. Your email service and the receiving mailbox service also process the correspondence.

Keep your message limited to what is needed. Do not send credentials, payment-card details or identity documents. You can use the same address to request access to, correction of or deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable obligations.

Storage, deletion and your choices

Local game records remain in the app’s storage until they are replaced through gameplay or removed with the app’s data. The game does not provide its own cross-device synchronization. Device backups and system services may process app data according to your device settings and their own policies.

Deleting the app’s local data can remove progress and practice work. Restoring an App Store purchase restores eligibility for paid content, not the deleted game save. Apple maintains its own purchase records; requests about those records should be directed to Apple. Support correspondence is kept as needed to handle your request and any applicable recordkeeping obligations.

Children and changes

The iOS game does not ask players to provide an age or register a personal account. A parent or guardian can contact us about information sent to support by a child. If the app’s processing changes, we will update this policy.

This support website

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The pages do not contain analytics scripts, advertising, forms or developer-set cookies. GitHub processes connection and request information when serving the site, including visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub’s privacy statement. This website hosting is separate from the app’s local game storage.

简体中文

本政策适用于 chen zhifeng 提供的《雨巷修伞铺》iOS App Store 版（Bundle ID：com.chenzhifeng.raincheckworkshop）。本政策不适用于 Android 广告版，也不替代 Apple 自身服务的隐私说明。

游戏数据保存在应用本机存储中

游戏将修伞进度、布片布局、已完成作品、设置及练习作品保存在应用的本地存储中。游戏没有独立游戏账号注册、开发者运营的云存档服务，也没有上传上述记录的功能。游戏不会将这些记录发送给我们。

本版不含广告、第三方分析 SDK 或由开发者运营的崩溃报告上传服务。游戏玩法不需要获取你的姓名、电子邮箱、通讯录、位置、相机、麦克风或广告标识符。

购买与 Apple 服务

完整版购买及恢复由 Apple 的 StoreKit 服务处理。游戏读取经过 Apple 验证的权益和交易信息，包括商品标识、交易标识、购买环境及权益状态，并在本机保留交付记录，以处理解锁中断后的核对。游戏不会将这份本地记录发送至开发者服务器。

我们不会通过游戏收到你的银行卡详情或 Apple 账户密码。Apple 依照其条款及 App Store 隐私说明处理购买、账户与设备信息。Apple 可能向开发者提供购买／退款记录和汇总报表。Apple 的处理与游戏的本地存储是不同的环节。

主动联系支持时

当你主动发送邮件至 610186215@qq.com 时，我们会收到发件地址和你自行提供的内容，例如问题描述、设备信息及可选截图。我们使用这些信息回复请求和处理问题。你的邮件服务及收件邮箱服务也会处理这封邮件。

请只提供处理问题所需的内容，不要发送登录凭证、银行卡信息或身份证件。你可以通过同一邮箱申请访问、更正或删除支持往来信息；我们会结合适用的义务处理。

保存、删除与选择

本机游戏记录保存在应用存储中，直至被后续游戏操作替换，或随应用数据被删除。游戏不提供自己的跨设备同步。设备备份及系统服务可能依照设备设置和各自政策处理应用数据。

删除应用本地数据可能清除进度与练习作品。恢复 App Store 购买只能恢复付费内容使用资格，不能找回已删除的游戏存档。Apple 自行保留其购买记录，相关请求应向 Apple 提出。支持往来信息仅在处理请求及履行适用记录保存义务所需的时间内保留。

儿童与政策更新

iOS 游戏不会要求玩家填写年龄或注册个人账号。若儿童向支持邮箱发送了信息，监护人可以联系我们处理。如果游戏的信息处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本政策。

本支持网站

本静态网站由 GitHub Pages 托管。页面没有分析脚本、广告、表单或开发者设置的 Cookie。GitHub 提供网页时会处理连接及请求信息，包括用于安全目的的访问者 IP 地址。详情请参阅 GitHub 隐私声明。网站托管与游戏的本机存储是不同的环节。
