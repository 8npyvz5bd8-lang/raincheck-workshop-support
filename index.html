English

Tell us what happened

You can include your platform, game version, device model and a short description of the problem. A screenshot is optional; remove personal information before sending it. Please never send passwords, payment-card details or identity documents.

Restore an iOS purchase

Open “Full story / Restore purchases” and choose “Restore purchases” while using the App Store account that made the purchase. Restoration checks full-story access; it does not restore deleted local progress or saved practice pieces.

If a purchase is pending approval, wait for Apple’s result. If the game says the verified unlock has not been safely saved, use “Retry saving unlock” rather than buying again.

Local saves

Progress and practice pieces are stored on your device. If saving fails, keep the game open and use its retry option. Deleting the app or its local data can erase progress. We do not have a separate game-save server from which to recover it.

Privacy questions

See the iOS privacy policy. Please identify the edition when contacting us: this policy covers the App Store edition only.

简体中文

说明遇到的问题

可以附上平台、游戏版本、设备型号和简短的问题描述。截图为可选项，发送前请遮去个人信息。请勿发送密码、银行卡信息或身份证件。

恢复 iOS 购买

使用原购买所用的 App Store 账户，打开“完整版／恢复购买”，再选择“恢复购买”。该操作用于查询完整版权益，不会找回已删除的本机游戏进度或练习作品。

若购买正在等待批准，请等待 Apple 返回结果。若游戏提示购买已验证但尚未安全保存，请选择“重试保存解锁结果”，无需再次购买。

本机存档

游戏进度与练习作品保存在设备上。保存失败时，请保留当前游戏并使用重试选项。删除应用或本地数据可能导致进度丢失。我们没有可用于找回这些存档的独立游戏存档服务器。

隐私问题

请阅读 iOS 隐私政策。联系我们时请说明游戏版本所在的平台；该政策仅适用于 App Store 版。
